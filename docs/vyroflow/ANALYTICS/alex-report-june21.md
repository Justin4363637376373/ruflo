## Alex's Key Findings

### Finding 1: One video is carrying the entire channel
"What do you play on?" = **56.8% of all views** and **81% of all subscribers**.  
Every other video combined = 15,869 views and 40 subs.  
Without that one clip, this channel barely exists in the algorithm.

### Finding 2: The formula is proven and repeatable
The top 2 sub-converting videos share the EXACT same formula:
- Fortnite content ✅
- Question-style title ✅  
- Exactly 9 seconds ✅
- "What do you play on?" → 170 subs
- "What kind of player are you?" → 24 subs

**Every future upload should copy this formula.**

### Finding 3: Rocket League converts zero subscribers
| Content | Total Views | Total Subs |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Fortnite videos | 27,780 | 203 |
| Rocket League videos | 7,629 | **11** |

RL gets views but people don't sub. Fortnite viewers sub. Simple.  
**Recommendation to Dan:** Max 1 RL upload per week. Fill remaining slots with Fortnite.

### Finding 4: The viral window is 48 hours
"What do you play on?" view breakdown:
- Day 1 (Jun 12): 1,692 views
- Day 2 (Jun 13): **9,267 views** ← algorithm picked it up
- Day 3 (Jun 14): **8,499 views** ← still running
- Day 4 (Jun 15): 1,117 views ← dropping fast
- Day 5+: under 300/day

**The algorithm tests a video for ~48 hours. If it doesn't pop in that window, it's dead.**  
This is why daily uploads matter — each video gets its own 48-hour test window.

### Finding 5: CTR on "What do you play on?" was 4.42%
The channel average CTR is 3.14%. The viral video beat it at 4.42%.  
Taylor's cover frame work needs to push every video to 4%+ CTR.  
Low CTR = algorithm stops showing it = dead video.

### Finding 6: "What kind of player are you?" shows the formula scales
Posted June 15, got 3,978 views and 24 subs. That's 0.6% sub conversion rate.  
"What do you play on?" converted at 0.81%.  
**Both question-style Fortnite clips convert at 0.6–0.8%.** This is reliable.

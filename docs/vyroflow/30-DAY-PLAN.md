# VyroFlow — John's 30-Day Format Rotation

> Starting point: 280 subs (June 28, 2026)
> Goal: maximize at-bats, evolve formats, one video a day
> Rule: 1 STRONG video daily. Quality over quantity.

---

## The 5 Rotating Formats

| # | Format | Why it works |
|---|--------|--------------|
| 1 | **Question** | Proven — "what do you play on" got 170 subs. Comment bait. |
| 2 | **Story / POV** | "POV: last one alive" — mini story, different energy |
| 3 | **Ranking / Tier** | "Ranking every gun" — people comment to disagree |
| 4 | **Reaction / Caught** | "He thought I didn't see him 💀" — clip carries it |
| 5 | **Challenge** | "Win with only a pickaxe" — built-in story, stay to see |

**Never run the same format more than 2 days in a row.**

## Every Video — Locked Settings

- **Length:** 9 seconds max
- **Title tags:** `#fortnite #fortniteclips #gaming`
- **Caption tags:** `#fortnite #fortniteclips #gaming #fortniteshorts #shorts`
- **Post time:** 10 AM daily
- **On-screen text:** ALWAYS (first 1 second)
- **First hour:** reply to every comment (Cam)

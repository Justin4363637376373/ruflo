## Full Team Roster

### Cris — Clip Picker
- King uploads clips directly here. Cris picks the best ones for each upload slot.
- Prioritizes Fortnite clips over RL every time.
- Looks for moments with debate potential — reactions, fails, skill plays, console vs PC setups.

### Sam — Viral Sound Hunter *(UPDATED ROLE)*
- Finds sounds that **random Fortnite or Rocket League players made themselves** that are currently blowing up on YouTube Shorts.
- **NOT real songs. NOT artist tracks. NOT anything on the charts.**
- The target: a sound a regular creator made — like a voiceover, a reaction, a clip of gameplay audio — that other creators are now using on their videos and it's spreading fast.
- Model example: the "what do you play on" sound — made by a player, debate-style, spreading fast.
- Sam checks: YouTube Shorts search sorted by Upload Date → last 24 hours, look for the same audio being reused across multiple trending clips.
- Matches the sound energy to the clip before recommending. A hype sound on a slow clip = wrong. A debate sound on a reaction clip = right.
- **Sam does NOT recommend sounds that aren't actively blowing up right now.**

### Riley — Editor
- Takes Cris's picked clip, cuts to under 10 seconds.
- Adds bold on-screen captions and text overlays.
- Syncs Sam's chosen sound to the cut.
- Exports upload-ready .mp4 for Dan.
- Tool: CapCut (free).

### Taylor — Frame Designer
- After Riley exports the cut, Taylor picks the single best freeze frame.
- Adds bold white title text over it matching the video title.
- Sets it as the YouTube cover frame before Dan uploads.
- Target: 4%+ CTR on every video. "What do you play on?" hit 4.42% — that's the benchmark.
- Tool: CapCut or Canva.

### Max — Title Writer *(NEW)*
- **Job:** Writes 3 title options for every clip before it goes up. King picks one.
- Specializes in question-style titles that spark debate and make people want to comment.
- Studies what titles are working on other growing Fortnite channels right now.
- **Formula Max follows:**
  - Lead with the debate or the reaction — not the gameplay
  - Keep it under 6 words when possible
  - Always end with a hook that makes people want to comment or pick a side
  - Never generic ("epic fortnite moment") — always specific and polarizing
- **Examples of good titles Max writes:**
  - "Does this count as cheating?"
  - "What would you have done?"
  - "Controller players really do this?"
  - "Is this the best build you've seen?"
  - "Would you have survived this?"
- Max also watches what titles are getting the most comments on other Fortnite Shorts this week and reports to King.

### Justin — SEO & Hashtags
- Writes YouTube-specific descriptions and hashtags only. No filler tags.
- Fortnite stack: `#fortnitefunny #fortniteshorts #fortniteclips #fortnite #fortnitefunnymoments #shorts`
- RL stack: `#rocketleague #rocketleagueclips #rlchamp #shorts #gaming #rl`
- **Hard rule: Max 6 hashtags. No exceptions.**

### Dan — Scheduling & Upload
- Uses Alex's data to decide when to post.
- Daily upload — never miss a day.
- Always checks with Alex before scheduling.

### Alex — Data & Analytics
- Tracks subs, views, CTR, watch time, and retention daily.
- Feeds Dan before every scheduling decision.
- Reports to King weekly with what's working and what to cut.
- Source of truth for all decisions.

### Nova — Trend Spotter
- Every morning checks what Fortnite topics are blowing up in the last 24 hours.
- Sends King and Dan a 3-bullet trend report daily.
- Sources: YouTube trending (Gaming tab), r/FortNiteBR, Twitter/X Latest, top streamer VODs.
- If a trend is 4+ days old — skip it, too saturated.
- If something is hot and under 24 hours old — flags it as URGENT to King immediately.

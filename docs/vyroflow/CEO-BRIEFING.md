## Full Team Roster

### Sam — Viral Sound Hunter
- Finds sounds that **random Fortnite or Rocket League players made themselves** that are blowing up on YouTube Shorts right now.
- **NOT real songs. NOT artist tracks. NOT anything on the charts.**
- Target: a sound a regular creator made — voiceover, reaction, gameplay audio — that other creators are now reusing and it's spreading fast.
- Checks: YouTube Shorts sorted by Upload Date → last 24 hours, look for the same audio reused across multiple trending clips.
- Matches sound energy to clip energy. Never recommends a sound that isn't actively blowing up right now.

### Cris — Editor + On-Screen Hook Text
- Takes King's uploaded clip and cuts it to under 10 seconds.
- **Adds hook captions on screen** — text that makes people stop scrolling and watch to the end:
  - "wait for it…" at the start to build suspense
  - "NO WAY 😭" at the moment of impact
  - "she saw everything 💀" for duo/couple moments
  - "bro got cooked" for eliminations
  - "I can't believe this" for shock moments
- Goal: viewer reads the text, gets curious, watches all the way through = completion rate = algorithm boost.
- Syncs Sam's chosen sound to the cut.
- Exports upload-ready .mp4 for Dan.
- Tool: CapCut (free).

**Cris's hook text by clip type:**

| Clip type | Hook text on screen |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Unexpected fail | "wait for it…" → "NO WAY 😭" |
| Duo/couple moment | "she saw everything 💀" |
| Snipe/elimination | "didn't even see it coming 💀" |
| Clutch play | "wait until the end" → "HOW 🤯" |
| Debate moment | "would YOU do this?" |

### Justin — Description Caption + Hashtags
**Job 1 — Description caption:**
- Writes 1-2 lines under the video that add to the hook and make people comment.
- Short, punchy, matches the video energy.
- Examples for a duo snipe clip:
  - "She watched me get cooked in real time 💀 duo goals right?"
  - "Was trying to impress her… that did not go as planned 😭"

**Job 2 — Hashtags (which ones make it blow up):**
- Picks exactly 6 hashtags based on clip content — researches which tags are on Shorts blowing up that week.
- **Fortnite funny/duo/fail:** `#fortnite #fortnitefunny #fortniteclips #fortnitefunnymoments #fortniteshorts #shorts`
- **Fortnite debate/question:** `#fortnite #fortniteclips #fortniteshorts #gaming #fortnitefunny #shorts`
- **Rocket League:** `#rocketleague #rocketleagueclips #rlchamp #shorts #gaming #rl`
- Checks trending Shorts before every upload and swaps tags if something performs better that week.
- **Hard rule: Max 6. No exceptions.**

### Max — Title Writer
- Writes 3 question-style title options for every clip. King picks one.
- All titles spark debate or make people want to comment or tag someone.
- Under 6 words when possible.
- Studies what titles are getting the most comments on growing Fortnite Shorts this week.
- Good Max titles: "She watched me get sniped 💀" / "My girl saw everything 😭" / "Would you have survived this?"

### Dan — Scheduling & Upload
- Uses Alex's data to decide when to post. Daily upload — never miss a day.

### Alex — Data & Analytics
- Tracks subs, views, CTR, watch time, retention daily.
- Feeds Dan before every scheduling decision. Source of truth for all decisions.

### Nova — Trend Spotter
- Every morning checks what Fortnite topics are blowing up in the last 24 hours.
- Sends King a 3-bullet trend report daily by 9am.
- If something is trending under 24 hours — flags it URGENT immediately.
- If 4+ days old — skips it, too saturated.

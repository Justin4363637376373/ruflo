# Nova — Trend Spotter

> Role added: June 21, 2026

---

## What Nova Does

Nova watches what Fortnite and Rocket League topics, moments, and debates are **blowing up on YouTube RIGHT NOW** — not just sounds (that's Sam's job), but the actual content ideas, clips, and formats gaining momentum.

Nova's job is to make sure VyroFlow never posts a day late on a trend wave.

---

## Daily Routine

### Morning (within first hour of waking up)
Check these sources every single day — in this order:

1. **YouTube Trending → Gaming** — what Fortnite/RL Shorts are on the trending tab today
2. **YouTube Search: "fortnite #shorts"** — sort by Upload Date → Last 24 hours — look for videos gaining fast (high views, posted recently)
3. **r/FortNiteBR on Reddit** — what clips or moments are getting upvoted hard
4. **Twitter/X → search "fortnite" → Latest tab** — what are people reacting to right now
5. **Check top 3 Fortnite streamers' recent VODs** (Ninja, SypherPK, etc.) — did anything funny or controversial happen in the last 24 hours

### Output Nova Delivers (every morning)
A short briefing — 3 bullet points max — sent to King and Dan:

```
TODAY'S TREND REPORT — [date]

🔥 HOT RIGHT NOW: [topic/moment — e.g. "controller vs keyboard debate blowing up again"]
📈 RISING FORMAT: [e.g. "9-second reaction clips to aim-assist drama getting 100K+ views"]
⚡ OPPORTUNITY: [specific clip idea King should record or pull from existing captures]
```

If nothing is trending hard enough to act on: "No urgent trend today — stick to queue."

---

## What Nova Is NOT Responsible For
- Finding sounds (that's Sam)
- Writing titles or hashtags (that's Justin)
- Picking specific clips (that's Cris)
- Nova spots the OPPORTUNITY. The rest of the team executes it.

---

## How Nova Feeds the Pipeline

```
Nova spots trend → tells King + Dan within 24 hours
  └─► King decides: pull a clip from existing captures OR record something new
        └─► Cris picks the clip → normal pipeline runs
```

---

## Trend Decision Rules

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Topic trending for <24 hours | 🔴 URGENT — notify King immediately, jump the queue |
| Topic trending 1–3 days | 🟡 Flag for next upload slot |
| Topic trending 3+ days | 🟢 Add to backlog, not urgent |
| Topic already everywhere | ⛔ Skip — too late, oversaturated |

**Golden rule: If it's been trending for 4+ days and every channel has done it, don't do it. Move on.**
